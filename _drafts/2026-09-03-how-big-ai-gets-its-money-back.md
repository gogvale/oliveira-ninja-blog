## The hook

Cheapest tokens ever, biggest buildout ever. Same quarter. Something has to give.

## The three theories

**1. Volume saves the token sellers.** Prices drop, usage explodes, total spend grows. Worked for memory, bandwidth, compute. The catch: business buyers are not consumers — their usage is capped by value, not appetite.

**2. The shovels get paid either way.** The buildout is the product. Chips, racks, power, buildings — the capex money lands there before a single token sells. If the AI bet fails, the shovel sellers still banked five years of record revenue.

**3. The survivors move up the stack.** Tokens commoditize; the money moves to agents, workflows, and outcomes sold as subscriptions. The labs become platforms. The blog already covered the buyer side of this ("Own the Skill. Rent the Tool.") — this is the seller side.

## The question I'm leaving open

Which theory do we believe? Each predicts something different we should see in the next 12-18 months: token volume curves, lab margins, who consolidates, what dies. I'm still watching which one the market proves.
